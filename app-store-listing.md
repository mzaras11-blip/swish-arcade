## Description (not search-indexed, but it converts views to downloads)

SWISH is a turn-based crossword battle. You and your opponent share one
arrowword grid. Place your five letter tiles before the 60-second timer
runs out, complete words to score, and stack bonuses to win.

• A brand-new puzzle generated for every game — no two matches alike
• Solo vs the computer (Easy, Normal, Hard) or pass-and-play with a friend
• Drag-and-drop tiles with 60-second turns
• Bonuses for correct letters, completed words, and blue squares
• Free hints when you're stuck
