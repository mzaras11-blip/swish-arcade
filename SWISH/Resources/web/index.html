1

Outscore your opponent

Place letters on the shared grid to complete words from the clues. Every letter and finished word adds to your score — finish with more points than the CPU to win.

Some clues are pictures: when a clue square shows one big picture instead of words, the picture is the clue. Tap the square any time to read that clue in words too.

2

Your turn: 60 seconds, 5 tiles

Each turn you get 5 letter tiles and 60 seconds. Tap a tile, then tap a grid square to place it. Dragging a tile onto a square works too.

3

Score big

+1 for every tile placed. Completing a word pays bonus points equal to the word’s length. Place all 5 tiles in one turn for +5 — and landing on a blue square doubles your whole turn score.

4

Power-ups

Stuck? Add +30 seconds with a time boost (twice per game), or reveal a letter with a hint (3 per game). Replace trades 1 to 5 tiles you can’t use for fresh ones (3 times per game), and undo takes a placement back.

Ads keep S.W.I.S.H - Crossword Battle ARCADE free: banners sit on the menus and the game-over screen, and a short ad can play between games — never during your turn. Rewarded ads are always your choice, and one Remove Ads purchase in the Shop turns banners and between-game ads off for good.

5

Daily Challenge & themes

Every day there is one Daily Challenge — the same puzzle for every player that day. Finish it to keep your streak going; your streak shows on the Home screen.

Coins also buy board and rack themes in the Shop. A theme only changes how the game looks, never how it plays. Sound effects can be turned off next to the music controls on the Home screen.

Friends show Online now in the Friends tab when they are in the app. When a game ends, tap Share Board on the game-over screen to send a picture of the final board.
